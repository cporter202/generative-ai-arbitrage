# Complete Model List & Pricing Comparison

This document provides a detailed breakdown of all generative AI models available through Kie.ai and their pricing compared to direct providers.

## 📹 Video Generation Models

### Google Veo 3.1 (Quality)
- **Provider:** Google DeepMind
- **Features:**
  - Cinematic motion
  - Strong prompt adherence
  - Synchronized audio output
  - Native 1080p resolution
  - High-quality video outputs
- **Kie.ai Pricing:** **$2.00 per 8-second video** (with audio) = **$0.25 per second**
- **Direct Pricing:** Not publicly available (Google's internal/limited access)
- **Savings:** Access to cutting-edge model at fraction of cost

### Google Veo 3.1 Fast
- **Provider:** Google DeepMind
- **Features:**
  - Faster rendering than Veo 3.1
  - Lower cost variant
  - Maintains quality standards
  - Synchronized audio
  - Smooth motion
  - Realistic scenes
- **Kie.ai Pricing:** **$0.40 per 8-second video** (with audio) = **$0.05 per second**
- **Direct Pricing:** $6.00 per 8-second video on Replicate.com and Fal.ai
- **Savings:** **~93% cheaper** than direct providers

### Runway Aleph
- **Provider:** Runway
- **Features:**
  - Multi-task video editing
  - Add/remove objects
  - Relight footage
  - Change angles/styles via text
  - Advanced scene reasoning
  - Precise camera control
- **Kie.ai Access:** ✅ Available (pay-per-use)
- **Direct Access:** Runway subscription ($12-95/month)
- **Savings:** Pay only for what you use vs. monthly subscription

### Sora 2
- **Provider:** OpenAI
- **Features:**
  - High-quality HD video generation
  - Audio included
  - No watermarks
- **Kie.ai Pricing:** $0.15 per 10-second HD video
- **Direct Pricing:** 
  - Standard: $0.10/second = $1.00 per 10 seconds
  - Sora 2 Pro: $0.20/second = $2.00 per 10 seconds
- **Savings:** 85% (vs. standard) / 92.5% (vs. Pro)

### Seedance 1.5 Pro
- **Provider:** Various
- **Features:**
  - High-quality video generation
  - 480p output
- **Kie.ai Pricing:** $0.04 per 4-second video (480p, no audio)
- **Direct Pricing:** $0.052/second on Replicate = $0.208 per 4 seconds
- **Savings:** ~81% cheaper
